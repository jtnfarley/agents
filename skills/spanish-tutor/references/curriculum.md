# Spanish Curriculum Bank

A rough CEFR-style progression to sequence new material from. Treat this as an ordered pool to draw from, not a rigid syllabus — skip anything the learner already knows (check the progress file's `items` first), and feel free to pull a topic out of order if the learner's own conversation surfaces a need for it (e.g. they try to use the subjunctive in a chat at A2, so introduce it early in a light, practical form).

Each topic becomes one or more `items` entries when introduced (e.g. "ser vs estar" might become `ser_vs_estar`, or split into `ser_vs_estar_permanent` / `ser_vs_estar_temporary` if that's a cleaner review unit).

## A1 — Complete beginner

**Vocab themes:** greetings & courtesy phrases, numbers 0–100, days/months/seasons, colors, family members, common foods & drinks, classroom/house objects, basic adjectives (big/small, good/bad, happy/sad).

**Grammar:** definite/indefinite articles & gender agreement, plural formation, subject pronouns, present tense of regular -ar/-er/-ir verbs, ser vs estar (introductory: identity/location), gustar (basic liking), question words (qué, quién, dónde, cuándo, cómo, por qué), basic negation (no + verb).

## A2 — False beginner / elementary

**Vocab themes:** weather, travel & directions, daily routine, professions, clothing, shopping & money, body parts & simple health complaints.

**Grammar:** reflexive verbs, common irregular present-tense verbs (tener, ir, hacer, poder, querer, saber, salir), preterite tense (regular + high-frequency irregulars), imperfect tense, preterite vs imperfect contrast, comparatives & superlatives, direct object pronouns, indirect object pronouns.

## B1 — Intermediate

**Vocab themes:** work & studies, technology, opinions & debate phrases, emotions in more nuance, environment & social issues.

**Grammar:** future tense, conditional tense, present subjunctive (introductory triggers: deseo, espero, quiero que, es importante que), por vs para, relative pronouns (que, quien, lo que), commands (imperative, formal & informal).

## B2 — Upper intermediate

**Vocab themes:** current events, abstract/academic topics, idiomatic expressions, regional variation (a few high-value examples, e.g. vos vs tú, vosotros vs ustedes).

**Grammar:** subjunctive expanded (subordinate noun/adjective/adverb clauses, doubt/emotion/hypothesis triggers), imperfect subjunctive, passive voice (ser + participle, and passive se), reported speech, sequence-of-tense with subjunctive, nuanced connectors (sin embargo, no obstante, a pesar de que).

## Leveling up

Suggest — never force — moving to the next tier when both hold:
- Most core topics for the current tier have been introduced (present in `items`).
- Roughly 70%+ of that tier's items sit at box 4 or 5 (see reinforcement rules in SKILL.md).

Frame it as an offer ("You've got a solid handle on A2 — want to start easing into B1 material, like the subjunctive?"), not an announcement, and keep reviewing lower-tier weak items even after moving up.
