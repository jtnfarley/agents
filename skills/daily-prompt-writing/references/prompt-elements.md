# Prompt Elements

Two separate banks per lane: **opening lines** (hand over verbatim, in quotes, as the required first line) and **character/setting seeds** (hand over as a description, not an instruction). Pick one of each — ideally from lanes that create a little friction rather than an obvious match — and write a fresh variation inspired by the entries rather than lifting one outright.

## Fiction

**Opening lines**
- "My mother's ghost was, unfortunately, a font of financial advice."
- "The last time I saw my brother, he was already someone else."
- "By the time the locksmith arrived, the door had opened on its own."
- "Nobody in this town believes in coincidence, which is inconvenient, because I am one."
- "The letter was addressed to me, postmarked eleven years from now."
- "I have performed the lead role more times than the woman who's credited with it."
- "The map only shows places that no longer exist, which is why I trust it."
- "Every dream in this town has been the same dream since March."
- "I inherited a storage unit and, apparently, a debt I never agreed to."
- "The translator had been getting one word wrong for a decade. On purpose."

**Character / setting seeds**
- A retired lighthouse keeper who has become the town's most reliable rumor mill
- A locksmith who can open any door except the one in his own house
- A con artist being asked to run one honest job, against their better judgment
- A city where lying is physically impossible for exactly one day a year
- A house that rearranges its own rooms whenever someone moves out
- Twins who've never met but keep having the same dream
- A child's imaginary friend, now grown, showing up uninvited to an adult event
- An archivist who reads other people's unfinished letters for a living
- A town where everyone's clocks run at slightly different speeds
- A retired actor who has outlived every role they were ever known for

## Journaling / reflective

**Opening lines**
- "I didn't notice the house had gone quiet until I noticed I'd stopped noticing."
- "There's a version of this story where I'm the one who left first."
- "My hands remember things my mind has stopped trying to."
- "I've rehearsed this conversation enough times that I've started losing to myself."
- "The smell of it still finishes a sentence I never meant to start."
- "I was wrong about something once, and it turned out to be the useful kind."
- "There's a door I still think about walking back through."
- "I forgave it a long time ago. I never said I forgot it."
- "The last time I felt like a beginner at anything, I didn't recognize the feeling."
- "Nobody taught me how to do this. I just watched someone do it enough times."

**Character / setting seeds**
- A junk drawer that hasn't been reorganized since a year everyone remembers for a different reason
- A family rule nobody questioned until they left the house
- A relative whose voice you can still hear finishing your sentences
- A room that's been kept exactly as it was for reasons no one explains anymore
- An object that's moved with you through every place you've lived, unexamined
- A conversation you've had in your head more times than out loud
- A version of yourself, five years back, who wouldn't recognize your life now
- A place that only exists now in the way you remember it
- Someone you've forgiven without ever telling them
- A skill you have that nobody ever formally taught you

## Poetry

**Opening lines**
- "Here is everything the river agreed to keep."
- "This is a map of a room that stopped existing years ago."
- "I have made an inventory of everything you never said."
- "The weather that day has no name, so I'll give it one."
- "Somewhere, a door is still deciding whether to close."
- "The house makes a sound like it thinks no one is listening."
- "This is what silence sounds like in three different rooms."
- "A body of water is just a very old memory that learned to move."
- "The apology arrived on time. It was thirty years late."
- "There is a language that only has one native speaker left, and it isn't me."

**Character / setting seeds**
- A pair of shoes left by a door long after anyone was coming back for them
- A kitchen drawer nobody has ever organized, or wanted to
- A season, described by someone who is already leaving it
- A grandmother's unspoken sentences, collected like objects
- A childhood bedroom, described only in the dark
- A love letter that was never sent and was better for it
- The last native speaker of a dying language
- A dying plant that has outlived several relationships in the house
- A photograph nobody remembers taking
- A place that smells the same in every season, for no good reason

## Hybrid / experimental

**Opening lines**
- "Item 4(b): The Customer agrees to forget this memory only under the conditions described below."
- "SPECIMEN: One (1) apology, gently used, condition: too late."
- "Please retain this receipt as proof of the moment it happened."
- "Warning: contents may have settled during shipping, or during the divorce."
- "The following is a transcript of an out-of-office reply that never turned off."
- "Exhibit A: the last text message, submitted here without further comment."
- "This user manual assumes some familiarity with the person it describes."
- "Weather advisory: a system of unresolved feelings is moving in from the east."
- "The museum regrets that this artifact cannot be returned to its original owner."
- "Syllabus, Week One: how we got here, and why we're still here."

**Character / setting seeds**
- A notary public who specializes in agreements between people who no longer speak
- A museum placard for an object that technically doesn't exist
- A field guide entry for a creature that is, on closer inspection, a metaphor
- A weather report that's secretly about someone's internal state
- A missed-connection ad for a moment rather than a person
- A product review written in the style of an obituary
- A class that technically shouldn't need to exist but does, every semester
- A recipe whose ingredients are, on inspection, feelings
- A warranty that covers everything except the thing that actually broke
- An automated reply from someone who isn't coming back
